### Integration rate
- 10-K FY2019, FY2020, FY2022: "about 80% of our production is converted into corrugated packaging and other packaging by our ... North American corrugated packaging plants" (stable since ~2012; FY2004 said ~70%).
- After the 2025 Georgia closures (~1.43M t gross) the system is tighter; IP management describes the NA business as fully vertically integrated. No new percentage disclosed in retrieved FY2024/FY2025 excerpts.

### Shipping radius
- Not disclosed in retrieved excerpts. [knowledge] Industry norm cited by PCA/WestRock/AF&PA material: corrugated plants typically serve customers within roughly 150–250 miles because boxes are bulky/low-value per cubic foot; containerboard rolls travel much farther (mill-to-box-plant hauls of 300–600+ miles and export are common).

### Maintenance outages
- IP reports planned maintenance (outage) expense by quarter in earnings materials; outages are front-loaded into Q1–Q2 and Q4 is lightest. [knowledge: format]
- 2026 (retrieved): analyst preview put expected Q2-2026 outage expense at ~$214M (mostly Packaging Solutions North America), almost half of ~$442M projected for the full year. On the Q2-2026 call IP said Q2 was "a very heavy outage quarter at roughly twice our normal levels"; outage costs were ~$127M unfavorable vs Q2-2025. Q1-2026 outage costs were lower because one outage was deferred into Q2.
- Unplanned: Pine Hill AL mill storm/roof damage (mid-2026) — ~$85M pre-insurance Q3-2026 impact; total H2 disruption $70–100M, mostly expected to be recovered via insurance. (Sources: paperage.com Q1/Q2 2026 results, Yahoo/Investing.com call coverage.)
- Tons lost per outage are not disclosed; [knowledge] a typical annual cold outage is 7–14 days per machine/mill, i.e., roughly 2–4% of annual capacity.

### Capacity anchor
- 10-K FY2024 Appendix II: U.S. containerboard capacity 12,984 thousand short tons (EMEA 560, Americas other than US 27; total 13,571); footnote says the line includes saturated kraft, kraft bag and gypsum facing paper, and includes the Campti LA mill closed in 2025.
- Oct 2023 8-K (Orange closure): remaining NA system "17 mills with annual production capability of 13MM tons".
- 2025 actions: Campti (−800k, Apr 2025), Riceboro (−430k, Sep 2025), Savannah (−1,000k, Sep 2025), Riverdale PM16 conversion (+undisclosed, completed Aug 2026; ~+430k implied by IP's "net ~1.0M t reduction"), NORPAC Longview acquisition (Jun 2026; ~1.0M t of containerboard and other grades, 3 PMs).

## Smurfit Westrock (SW)

### Plant counts
- 10-K FY2025: 152 corrugated packaging plants in North America; 27 mills in North America (containerboard + paperboard + Canada + Mexico).
- 10-K FY2024: globally 62 paper mills and 459 converting plants (306 of which convert containerboard into corrugated containers); North America containerboard mills produced ~8.2 million tons in 2024 (including WestRock tons produced before the July 2024 combination).
- 10-K FY2025 excerpt: North America containerboard output ~9.3 million tons in 2025 (search-tool summary; verify — may include other grades).
- Smurfit Kappa Mexico (2018 article): 3 containerboard mills (Cerro Gordo, Los Reyes, Monterrey) and 15 converting plants in Mexico. WestRock added Grupo Gondi (Monterrey, San Luis Potosí mills + converting) in 2022.
- Converting closures: two German plants (2025), City of Industry CA (141 workers, 2025), eight further converting facilities in Europe and North America announced on the Q2-2026 call. Chile: agreement in 2026 to acquire CMPC's Chilean containerboard/corrugated business (Latin America segment).

### Integration rate
- 10-K FY2025: "the majority" of mill output is consumed by the company's own converting operations, remainder sold externally — no percentage given.
- Fastmarkets (pre-merger, 2023): WestRock's containerboard capacity was ~1.4 million tonnes (23%) above its own corrugating plants' needs → implied legacy-WestRock integration ≈ 77–80%; Smurfit Kappa Europe historically ~ 100%+ integrated. [knowledge] Post-2025 closures (Forney, Mexico machine) were explicitly aimed at raising NA integration.
- WestRock FY2023 10-K: "capacity to annually ship approximately 10.6 million tons from its containerboard mills" (pre-closure, includes Brazil).

### Shipping radius
- Not found in retrieved excerpts. [knowledge] WestRock/PCA-style disclosure: box plants generally serve customers within ~150 miles; SW emphasises local-market service.

### Maintenance / downtime practice
- No tonnage schedule of planned maintenance outages was retrieved. [knowledge] Legacy WestRock disclosed scheduled mill maintenance downtime (thousand tons per quarter) in quarterly earnings decks; SW has so far framed downtime in dollar terms.
- Q3-2025 call: additional economic downtime in Q4-2025, "primarily in North America", estimated $60–70M EBITDA impact; FY2025 adjusted EBITDA guidance trimmed to $4.9–5.1B.
- Q1-2026: lower volumes, economic downtime, adverse weather and freight weighed on results; Q2-2026 10-Q: net income helped by lower downtime vs prior-year quarter; management said it was "nearly sold out of most grades" in a tight market after ~10% of NA industry capacity was closed in 2025.
- Capacity actions: Forney TX containerboard mill + St. Paul MN CRB mill (>500k t combined, announced 30 Apr 2025); with "a machine in Mexico and another in the Netherlands" total ≈ 600k t; La Tuque QC SBS machine 127k t (Feb 2026); Birmingham UK SSK mill ~200k t fluting/liner (2026, EMEA).
