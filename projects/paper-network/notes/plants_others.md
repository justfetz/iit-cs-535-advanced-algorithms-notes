# Corrugated plant networks, integration, shipping radius and outage practice
## Non-IP / non-Smurfit Westrock North American containerboard producers (status Oct 2026)

Compiled 2026-10-20 from web-search excerpts (SEC filings, company releases, Packaging Dive, Recycling Today,
Resource Recycling, Fastmarkets, local press). Direct fetches of sec.gov, packagingcorp.com, gp.com, prattindustries.com
etc. were DNS-blocked in this environment, so state-by-state plant tables could not be pulled verbatim; what follows is what
the search excerpts quoted, plus clearly flagged background knowledge.

---

## 1. Packaging Corporation of America (PKG)

**Mill / plant counts**
- 10-K FY2025 and Q4-2025 release: **10 containerboard mills (incl. the two ex-Greif mills) and 91 corrugated products
  plants** at 31 Dec 2025. The Q3-2025 release cited 93 plants, so two closed in Q4 2025. PCA's investor page still says
  "nine containerboard mills and 91 converting operations" (counts Jackson/International Falls differently).
  Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/75677/000119312526074129/pkg-20251231.htm ;
  https://ir.packagingcorp.com/news-releases/news-release-details/packaging-corporation-america-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-14
- 10-K FY2025 Item 2 says the facility list and map are presented in Part I, Item 1 (Business); the table could not be
  retrieved here. Older filings: "corrugated manufacturing operations in 26 states" (early-2000s 10-Ks) and
  "32 states" (10-K FY2013). Mill-by-mill capacities were last disclosed in the FY2008-FY2010 10-Ks (AF&PA basis) and
  for DeRidder in FY2016.
- 2025: PCA closed a Georgia corrugated plant (103 employees)
  (https://www.packagingdive.com/news/packaging-corporation-america-georgia-corrugated-plant-closure-layoffs/737077/).
- Greif deal (closed 2 Sep 2025, $1.8B) added the Riverville VA and Massillon OH mills plus Greif's eight sheet-feeder /
  corrugated plants (background knowledge; count not re-verified).

**Integration**
- Older 10-Ks: "our 65 corrugated products plants consume approximately 80% of our mills' containerboard production";
  a later filing with 71 plants again says ~80%. No 2025 percentage found; PCA remains one of the most integrated producers
  (background: ~85-90% of mill tons go to own plants in recent years; treat as unverified).
- Production: 5,046 kt in 2024 (user-supplied); 5.2 Mt (305 BSF) in 2025; Q2 2026 production 1,415 kt.
  Capacity ~5.8 Mt (358 BSF) end-2025 vs ~301 BSF end-2024.

**Shipping radius**
- PCA 10-K (c. 2021): corrugated plants "each serve a market radius of approximately 150 miles"
  (https://ir.packagingcorp.com/static-files/80b09ed5-7e04-48ae-8d80-ce6af2b0c452). A trade guide cites 200-300 miles
  as the typical truck radius for finished boxes (secondary).

**Maintenance outage practice (PCA)**
- PCA takes one scheduled annual maintenance outage per containerboard mill and discloses the quarterly EPS impact.
  2026 cadence from earnings releases/transcript:
  - Q1 2026: Counce TN outage; mill-outage expense $0.14/share; lower production at reconfigured Wallula; two fewer
    operating days.
  - Q2 2026: scheduled outages at **five** containerboard mills; outage expense projected $0.36/share (actual not seen);
    plus five unplanned utility-power outages costing ~10,000 t.
  - Q3 2026: "lower impact from maintenance outages"; Paper segment outage at International Falls.
  - Q4 2026: outage expense projected to rise again (details not found).
  Sources: https://ir.packagingcorp.com/news-releases/news-release-details/packaging-corporation-america-reports-first-quarter-2026-results ;
  https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/23/packaging-corp-pkg-q1-2026-earnings-transcript/ ;
  https://ir.packagingcorp.com/news-releases/news-release-details/packaging-corporation-america-reports-second-quarter-2026
- Massillon OH: post-acquisition outage extended to five weeks (late 2025) for upgrades
  (https://www.packagingdive.com/news/pca-q4-full-year-2025-earnings-mills-greif-acquisition/810670/).
- Wallula WA reconfiguration (announced late 2025): W2 and kraft pulping shut, ~200 jobs, W3 at 285 kt; the 250 kt
  reduction to be replaced by "production enhancements at other PCA mills beginning Q4 2026", including ~140 kt/yr of
  lightweight linerboard at Jackson AL in Q4 2026
  (https://ir.packagingcorp.com/news-releases/news-release-details/packaging-corporation-america-announces-reconfiguration-wallula).
- Background knowledge (unverified this session): a typical kraft mill annual outage is 7-14 days per machine/mill,
  i.e. roughly 2-4% of annual tons; PCA's mill-outage cost guidance has run ~$1.00-1.20/share per year in recent years.

## 2. Georgia-Pacific (Koch Industries, private)

- Corrugated network: at the May 2025 appointment of a new corrugated president GP described **35 sites** including box
  plants, sheet feeders, and digital print/mailing brands (https://finance.yahoo.com/news/georgia-pacific-shut-cedar-springs-082750368.html).
  User's "~30 corrugated plants" is consistent once sheet feeders/digital brands are netted out.
- Mt. Olive, IL box plant closing at end-2025 (134 employees)
  (https://www.packagingdive.com/news/georgia-pacific-close-illinois-box-plant-134-employees/804284/).
- Cedar Springs GA mill closed 1 Aug 2025 (~1.0 Mt, ~535 jobs; ~2.4% of US capacity per Jefferies/Citi notes). GP does not
  disclose mill capacities. Remaining containerboard mills: Toledo OR, Monticello MS (>1.0 Mt produced 2024), Brewton AL
  (white-top), Big Island VA (recycled). Port Hudson LA is pulp/tissue (not containerboard).
- Integration: not disclosed; GP is a large open-market seller of linerboard (background knowledge). The Toledo mill's
  Juno plant and OCC intake (~500 kt/yr) are company claims.
- Industry context: Fastmarkets says IP + GP 2025 closures removed ~2.5 Mt; Truist put announced/completed 2025 cuts at
  ~2.3 Mt (~5.5% of NA supply); Packaging Dive: "containerboard capacity has shrunk nearly 6% in 2025".

## 3. Pratt Industries (private)

- Six 100%-recycled mills (Conyers GA, Staten Island NY, Valparaiso IN, Shreveport LA, Wapakoneta OH, Henderson KY)
  confirmed in a June 2026 release (https://www.pulpapernews.com/20260619/17938/pratt-industries-expand-rockwall-technology-park).
- Fastmarkets RISI (2020): five mills with 1.91 Mt/yr capacity "nearly fully integrated to **70 Pratt corrugated plants,
  including 30 sheet plants**" (https://www.recyclingtoday.com/article/pratt-industries-us-containerboard-capacity-expansion/).
  With Henderson (1,500 tpd) total capacity is ~2.4-2.5 Mt/yr (this compilation: 2,435 kt).
- 2025 White House "Invest in America" pledge: $5B over ten years, ~5,000 jobs; named projects include the Henderson KY
  mill/box plant campus and a $120M facility in Warner Robins GA. June 2026: $90M, 346,000 sq ft expansion of the Rockwall
  TX box plant (100 jobs). No seventh mill announced as of these sources.
- Integration: Pratt describes its system as essentially fully integrated (mills feed own box/sheet plants; Pratt Recycling
  MRFs feed mills). Shipping radius not stated.

## 4. Green Bay Packaging (private)

- Mills: Green Bay WI (685 kt, started Mar 2021) and Arkansas Kraft, Morrilton AR (two virgin machines, 420-440 kt in 2015;
  $1B "Project PowerPack" to Q1 2028, could double capacity; Truist: >1.03 Mt).
- Converting: GBP operates ~40 corrugated/sheet/folding plants across the US (background knowledge; site list at gbp.com
  could not be fetched). Arkansas Kraft output also sells to open market.
  Sources: https://www.packagingdive.com/news/green-bay-packaging-investment-expansion-kraft-linerboard-mill-arkansas/749834/ ;

## 5. Cascades (Containerboard Packaging)

- Mills after 2025: Bear Island VA (465 kt), Greenpac NY (540 kt), Cabano QC (270 kt), Kingsey Falls QC, Mississauga ON.
  Closed: Trenton ON medium mill (not restarted, Feb 2024) and Niagara Falls NY medium mill (200 kt, ended 3 Sep 2025).
- Feb 2024 restructuring also closed converting plants in Belleville ON and Newtown CT (310 employees across three sites);
  volume shifted to Bear Island (https://www.packagingdive.com/news/cascades-closures-containerboard-layoffs-optimization-connecticut-ontario/707344/).
- AIF 2022: six linerboard/medium mills with combined 1,575,000 short tons capacity. Cascades runs ~20 containerboard
  converting plants in Canada and the US northeast (background knowledge; AIF lists them but was not retrievable).

## 6. New-Indy Containerboard (Kraft Group / Schwarz Partners)

- Mills: Catawba SC (400 kt+ virgin kraft liner), Ontario CA, Oxnard CA, Hartford City IN (750 kt recycled combined).
  Schwarz Partners says the company makes "nearly one million tons" of containerboard.
- Converting: owners' affiliated networks (Rand-Whitney = Kraft Group box plants in the Northeast; Schwarz Partners' sheet
  feeders/box plants in the Midwest); New-Indy bought Shoreline Container (Michigan) in 2019
  (https://www.recyclingtoday.com/news/new-indy-containerboard-shoreline-michigan/). Plant counts not found.

## 7. Others (brief)

- **ND Paper (Nine Dragons):** Biron WI (B26 >500 kt; B25 idled Apr 2024, restart planned H2 2025 - unconfirmed), Rumford
  ME (300 kt packaging grades on R15), Fairmont WV (recycled pulp only, curtailed 2023), Old Town ME (pulp). No box plants.
- **Domtar Kingsport TN:** 600 kt recycled liner/medium; sells to open market (Domtar has no box plants). Domtar was still
  "evaluating" converting the Gatineau QC newsprint mill to containerboard as of Aug 2025 (union deal tied to it; no
  decision found) (https://www.euwid-paper.com/news/companies/domtars-conversion-of-gatineau-newsprint-mill-to-containerboard-still-under-review-290825/).
- **Hood Container:** St. Francisville LA kraft mill feeds ~14 Hood box/sheet plants in the Southeast (background knowledge).
  2025 release: $118.9M machine upgrade, +80 kt/yr; phase 1 Q3 2026, completion during the **May 2027 annual mill outage**
  (explicit statement that the mill takes an annual outage in May).
- **Port Townsend Paper:** one mill (~950-1,000 tpd incl. pulp); sister box plants (Crown Packaging / Crown Paper Group) in
  BC and Alberta ship into the Pacific Northwest (background knowledge).
- **McKinley Paper / Bio Pappel:** Prewitt NM (250 kt) open; Port Angeles WA (250 kt) idled Aug 2024, site sold Jun 2026;
  Combined Locks WI (ex-Midwest Paper Group, 3 machines, 500 kt all grades) operating (PM6 dryer fire 1 Jun 2026, no
  injuries). US box plants in the Southwest (McKinley Packaging) - count not found.
- **Liberty Paper / Liberty Diversified:** Becker MN mill (~800 tpd paper) supplies Liberty Packaging's box plants;
  steam from Xcel's Sherco coal plant (retiring 2026-2030) is the key 2026 risk.
- **Jackson Paper, Sylva NC:** ~110 kt medium, 100% recycled; sells to independents; planned Morganton NC sheet/box plant (2018).
- **Kruger:** Trois-Rivieres QC (375 kt metric, two machines; XTR linerboard) feeds Kruger box plants in LaSalle QC and
  Brampton ON, remainder open market; Place Turcot (158 kt metric; recycled white-top liner, specialty boards).
- **Atlantic Packaging:** Whitby ON (PM1 + 400 kt PM2) and Scarborough ON mills feed Atlantic's own Ontario corrugated plants.
- **Irving Lake Utopia NB:** 185 kt metric medium; supplies Irving's box plants in Atlantic Canada and New England.
- **NORPAC Longview WA:** ~1 Mt all grades, ~1/3 recycled packaging; **acquired by International Paper 4 Jun 2026 ($360M)**.
- **Crossroads Paper:** proposed 350 kt recycled mill moved from Utah to Winnemucca NV; county approval Aug 2025; not built.
- **Phoenix Paper (Shanying), Wickliffe KY:** still UFS + market pulp; the 2019 recycled containerboard/700 kt recycling
  project was not executed as far as sources show. Excluded.
- **Excluded by grade:** Sonoco Hartsville (URB), Graphic Packaging Texarkana (SBS), Clearwater (SBS), Kruger Brompton,
  Cascades Candiac (tissue), Greif URB/CRB mills, Newman/Ox/Newark (URB).
